Google Play Store Compliant Account & Data Deletion Policy

Account & Data Deletion Request

MediSense gives you complete control over your health information. Follow the instructions below to wipe your local cache or permanently delete your cloud account.

App Package: com.medisense.app
Deletion SLA: Within 30 Calendar Days

How to Delete Your Data

Depending on whether you wish to reset your phone's local storage or completely eliminate your cloud account from our servers, choose the corresponding method:

1

Method 1: Instant In-App Local Data Wipe (Self-Service)

If you currently have the MediSense app installed on your smartphone:

  1. Open MediSense on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to your Profile or tap the Settings icon.
  3. Select Privacy & Security.
  4. Tap the red button labeled "Clear Local Health Data".
  5. Confirm the dialog. All locally stored Room database tables (health profile, medications, appointments, prediction history, and chat logs) will be immediately and irreversibly wiped from device storage.
2

Method 2: Complete Cloud Account & Backend Purge (Permanent)

To permanently delete your cloud database records and authentication credentials from our PostgreSQL cloud database (Supabase), complete and submit the formal deletion request below.

This is available to all users, even if you have already uninstalled the application.

Irreversible Action

Once your cloud account and data have been purged, this action cannot be undone. If you wish to save your medical history prior to deletion, please use the Health Data Portability feature in the application to download your records as a JSON archive.
